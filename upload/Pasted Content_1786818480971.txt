23. 🏫 YOUTH + SCHOOL PRIVACY

This requires careful architecture.

COPPA applies to covered online services collecting personal information from children under 13 and generally places parents in control of collection of young children's personal information. The FTC finalized changes to the COPPA framework in 2025 and has continued issuing guidance on children's privacy and age-verification matters in 2026.

FERPA can also become relevant when information maintained by or for a covered educational institution constitutes an education record. The Department of Education defines education records broadly as records directly related to a student and maintained by the educational institution or a party acting for it.

If the tech team follows this architecture, GridironIQ becomes more than a playbook application. It becomes the operating system for teaching, measuring, retaining, and eventually analyzing an entire football program.

The first technical milestone should be the Coach → Assignment → Player Learning → Event Tracking → Mastery → Coach Dashboard loop. Once that loop is rock solid, everything else, including film, scouting, game planning, AI recommendations, and program memory, has a reliable foundation to build on.
